Phone Golf privacy policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Phone Golf counts the number of times that you pick up your phone each day. This policy tells you which data the app uses and where the data goes. Phone Golf is open source. You can read the code at github.com/ds17f/phone-golf.

Solo play

Solo play is the default. In solo play, all data stays on your phone. The app sends nothing to a server. The data on your phone is:

  • The name that you type, if you type one.
  • Your handicap.
  • Your count for each day.

How the app counts

Android: the app asks for usage access. The app uses this access only to count the times that you unlock the phone each day. The app does not read which apps you use.

iPhone: the app asks for Screen Time access. iOS shows your pickups in the app. The app cannot read this data, and the data does not leave your phone. For group play, you type the number.

Group play

Group play is an option. If you play with a group, the app sends this data to the Phone Golf server:

  • The name that you type. It does not have to be your real name.
  • Your handicap and your count for each day.
  • The groups and rounds that you make or join.
  • Your platform (iPhone, Android or web).

The server makes a secret player key and the app keeps it. The server keeps only a hash of the key. The game has no accounts, no email addresses and no passwords. The other players in your groups can see your name, your handicap and your scores.

The server writes standard web logs, which can include your IP address. The logs are used only to operate the server and to find problems.

What Phone Golf does not do

  • It does not show advertisements.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not sell data or give data to other companies.
  • It does not collect your location, your contacts or your app usage.

How to delete your data

Solo play: use "Start over" in Settings, or remove the app. This deletes all the data.

Group play: you can leave a group in the app. To delete your player and all your scores from the server, open an issue at github.com/ds17f/phone-golf/issues. Do not put your player key in the issue.

Children

Phone Golf is not made for children less than 13 years old.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page shows the new policy and a new effective date. The history of the page is public in the source repository.
